Our Medicines

Real, quality-assured generic medicines — sourced the honest way.

The Jan Aushadhi advantage

Every medicine we carry is sourced through the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) — India's national generic medicine network. That means:

✓ Manufactured only in WHO-GMP certified facilities
✓ Every batch tested in NABL-accredited labs
✓ Priced 50–80% lower than branded equivalents
✓ The same active ingredients as branded drugs

The scheme currently offers over 2,100 medicines and 315 surgical items across 29 therapeutic categories through more than 18,000 Jan Aushadhi Kendras nationwide.

How we keep medicines genuine

1

Wholesale sourcing

Medicines come directly from Jan Aushadhi Kendras and registered wholesale generic distributors.

2

Batch verification

Every batch number is logged and checked. We never accept medicines without clear, verifiable labels.

3

Sealed delivery

Medicine is dispensed sealed, with written dosage instructions in the patient's own language.
