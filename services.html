Our Services

Simple, honest healthcare designed for rural life.

All our services

💊

Genuine Medicines

We source only authentic, approved medicines and verify every batch. No fakes, ever. Medicines are delivered sealed, with clear labels and instructions.

🔬

Doctor Consultations

Consult qualified doctors for common illnesses, check-ups, and ongoing care — at our clinic or over a call when travel isn't possible.

🏠

Home Visits

Our care team comes to your home for elders, bedridden patients, and families who can't easily reach a clinic.

✏

Health Camps

Regular free check-up camps in villages covering blood pressure, sugar, vision, and general health awareness.

🌿

Awareness Programs

Workshops on nutrition, hygiene, water safety, and how to spot fake medicines — in your own language.

🚚

Emergency Support

A 24/7 helpline that guides you and arranges urgent help — ambulance, medicines, or a doctor — when you need it most.

👱

Specialist Referrals

Cardiology, diabetes, maternal, and orthopaedic specialists connected live for complex cases — you stay in your village.

🧩

Chronic Care Tracking

Monthly supply and monitoring for diabetes and blood pressure, so elders never miss a dose.

👦

Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal guidance, child check-ups, vaccinations, and nutrition support at every camp.

How it works — step by step

1

We arrive on schedule

The mobile clinic reaches your village on the same day every week, announced through the panchayat and health workers.

2

Vitals are recorded offline

The nurse logs your BP, sugar, and temperature on the clinic device — no network needed, and it stays private.

3

Severity decides the route

Green: treated on the spot. Yellow: sent to the town clinic as a data packet. Red: live-linked to a specialist immediately.

4

Medicines are dispensed

Genuine Jan Aushadhi medicines, sealed, with dosage instructions in your own language.

5

Follow-up & referrals

Chronic patients are tracked weekly. Hospital cases are arranged and accompanied from start to finish.
