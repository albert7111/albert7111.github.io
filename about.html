About Maruvaidyam

A calm helper you can count on when you're worried.

Our story

Maruvaidyam began with a simple question: why should people in villages have to choose between far-away care and untrustworthy medicines?

So we built a service that brings safe, reliable healthcare directly to rural communities. Our name comes from South Indian languages and carries the spirit of the region — the idea of vaidya, the healer, close to home.

We are not focused on any one tradition of medicine. We are focused on one thing: care that is safe, honest, and truly helps.

Our mission & vision

🚗

Mission

To put safe, reliable healthcare and genuine medicines within walking distance of every rural family — no matter how far the roads go.

🗻

Vision

A future where no one in a village ever has to choose between a long journey, a fake medicine, or no care at all.

Our core principles

Five principles guide every decision we make, from the medicines we stock to the way we greet you at the counter.

✓

Trust above all

Every medicine is verified at every step. Every word we speak is true — even when the truth is hard.

❤

Care like family

We treat every person with the patience, dignity, and warmth we would give our own parents and children.

🚗

Reach the last village

Healthcare should travel to the village, not the other way around. No terrain is too far for a Maruvaidyam unit.

💰

Honest prices

Genuine medicines at genuine prices. A nominal visit fee keeps us running — never profit from sickness.

☩

Stay calm

Worry is a sickness too. When you're scared, we stay calm, listen fully, and guide you step by step.

📱

Work offline-first

Our systems run on local data even where there is zero mobile network — so care never stops at a connectivity gap.

Our specialist doctors

Beyond our field doctors, we connect to specialist consultants through the central hub — both in person and by live referral for complex cases. You don't have to travel to town; the specialist comes to your village.

💔

Cardiology

Heart care and blood-pressure management, reviewed by specialists for high-risk patients.

🧩

Diabetes & Endocrine

Complex sugar control, insulin guidance, and thyroid support.

🤪

Maternal & Child Health

High-risk pregnancy monitoring and newborn follow-up with our obstetrician and paediatrician.

👔

Orthopedics

Joint, back, and fracture care — a top need for manual labourers and the elderly.

🧫

Dermatology

Skin and fungal conditions common in farm work, treated with photos and live review.

💊

Pulmonology

Asthma and COPD care, including inhaler technique training at the camp.

How it works — start to finish

1

We arrive on schedule

The mobile clinic reaches your village on the same day every week — announced through the panchayat and our health workers.

2

You meet the team

A nurse records your vitals offline — BP, sugar, temperature. Everything stays private on the local device.

3

Care is routed by severity

Green: mild case, treated on the spot. Yellow: sent to the town clinic as a data packet. Red: live-linked to a specialist immediately.

4

Real medicines, right there

Genuine Jan Aushadhi medicines are dispensed sealed, with dosage instructions in your own language.

5

We follow up

Chronic patients are tracked week to week. If you need a hospital, we arrange the referral and get you there.
